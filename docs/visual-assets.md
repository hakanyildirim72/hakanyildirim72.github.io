### Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one refined editorial image for the hero of an academic cybersecurity and engineering portfolio. A sculptural system of transparent optical-glass panels and dark navy metallic layers, connected by a small number of precise cyan light paths, suggesting cryptographic access, trustworthy information and secure systems. Sophisticated architectural studio render, restrained and intellectual, crisp material detail, soft volumetric light, deep midnight navy background with subtle blue illumination. Central sculptural composition, landscape 3:2 framing, enough quiet negative space around the object, suited to cropping on mobile. This is an abstract thematic illustration, not a literal research result or product. No people, no hooded hackers, no padlocks, no text, no letters, no numbers, no branding, no watermark. Palette matches navy #092b49, blue #0067a8 and restrained cyan #23c4ee.
